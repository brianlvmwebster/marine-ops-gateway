# Parts Inventory in Google staging

Lakeview has a Parts Inventory tool at `/inventory/parts`, available through Company workspace. The first version is a searchable catalog and opening stock snapshot. Search covers part/description, supplier, category, manufacturer, UPC and bins; filters include active/inactive and positive/zero/negative on-hand balances. Details show prices, costs, reorder values, all three bins and the original Lightspeed fields.

Migration 012 registers the Lakeview entitlement and creates company-scoped parts. Every part receives a Marine Ops UUID. Supplier code plus part number is unique within a company; numbers and UPCs stay text, including leading zeros. Quantities and prices retain exact decimal strings, including negative and fractional quantities. Available stock is separate from on hand. Inactive records remain searchable. No pricing, reservation or stock-adjustment rules are inferred from source flags or ambiguous headings.

The authorized export contains 24,097 records: 17,942 active and 6,155 inactive. All inactive records have zero on hand. There are 34 negative and five fractional on-hand balances. No source records are excluded. Blank layout rows and the report footer are not records. The full 127-cell source row is retained in column order, preserving repeated/truncated headings and original values.

`scripts/prepare-lightspeed-parts.py` reads legacy XLS with xlrd 2.0.2 and produces private JSON in ignored backups. `staging/import-parts.mjs` pins the staging host, imports bounded batches and verifies every mapped field and source-row hash, distinct UUIDs, counts and status/stock filters. Identical retries skip existing records. Conflicting supplier/part records reject and roll back the batch instead of overwriting. Imports require company owner/admin access, enabled tool access and same-origin requests. Read access requires active company membership and tool access. Audit events contain batch hashes/counts only.

Receiving and signed stock adjustments are available to company owners/admins. Catalog editing, serial tracking and purchasing remain subsequent work. Service Desk parts usage records explicit stock deductions; live inventory synchronization is not enabled. Runtime routes and deployment are staging-only. Live Floot, main branches and Outlook synchronization remain unchanged.

Administration provides the reviewed Lightspeed parts refresh. It preserves each part's original source row and opening values, and tracks the latest accepted mapped values separately. Quantity refreshes on parts without local movements have their own timestamp. See [Import update audit](IMPORT_AUDIT.md) for the review and application rules.

PostgreSQL CI covers authorization, inactive and fractional/negative balances, independent supplier keys, retry safety and conflicting-batch rollback. Source XLS/JSON and staging records must never enter Git or container build contexts.

## Receiving, adjustments and history

Migration 013 freezes the original mapped values in opening_data and adds versioned stock changes. Receipts require a positive quantity; adjustments accept a nonzero signed delta, including fractional quantities up to six decimal places. A reason is required and a receipt/reference is optional. Both on-hand and available stock change by the same delta, preserving the imported difference. Inactive parts may be corrected or received without changing their status. Costs and imported valuation are not recalculated.

A transaction locks the part, checks its version, updates exact PostgreSQL numeric balances and appends before/after values, server timestamp, actor ID/name, reason and reference. Repeated identical submission IDs are idempotent; conflicting or stale submissions are rejected. Database triggers prevent editing/deleting history; corrections are new movements. Original source rows and opening values remain unchanged. Imports compare opening values and never reset operational balances. History is paginated and available to authorized members; mutation requires owner/admin access. Staging changes are attributed to the shared Lakeview Test Owner login, not separate real people.

Use staging/migrate-stock.ts for the focused staging migration and staging/verify-part-stock.mjs with --seed then --verify-browser for the synthetic test workflow. The test fixture is separate from the 24,097 Lightspeed parts. Full import verification now reconciles original-row hashes and unchanged catalog fields while allowing later legitimate stock movements.

Stock workflow verified in staging revision `marine-ops-staging-00023-br6`: a synthetic part received 2.5 units, then a -1.25 adjustment. Current balances became 11.25 on hand and 9.25 available while opening balances stayed 10 and 8. Browser reopening and directory refresh retained balances and both history entries. API checks confirmed actor/reason/reference history and rejected stale versions, zero quantities, missing reasons and anonymous history reads. PostgreSQL CI passed decimal arithmetic, concurrent updates, idempotency, import preservation and immutable-ledger tests. All operational test changes were confined to the synthetic part.

## Service Desk job parts

Saved jobs expose a Parts used on this job section in the full job view, including jobs opened from customer/unit history. Search active inventory, choose a part and save its total job quantity with a reason. One line per job/part retains its supplier, number, description and initial price/cost snapshot. Pricing is a reference snapshot, not invoicing or inventory valuation. New jobs must be saved before adding parts.

Migration 014 adds service_job_parts and structured job links to immutable stock history. Adding/increasing a quantity deducts the difference from on hand and available; decreasing or setting zero returns the difference. Removed lines remain at zero for traceability. No reservations or deductions are triggered by merely searching, editing job details, printing or changing job status. Cancelled jobs permit returns only; completed/reviewed jobs are locked. Inactive parts cannot be newly issued, but existing quantities can be returned. Deductions require sufficient on-hand and available stock.

Changes require active Lakeview membership, Service Desk and Parts Inventory access, and company owner/admin authority. The transaction locks the job and part, checks line version and current stock, updates quantities and appends job number/ID, actor, reason and before/after balances. Idempotent submission IDs prevent double deductions. Concurrent jobs cannot overdraw the same available stock. Conflicts require refreshing job parts. Normal job saves cannot overwrite the separately stored parts.

Use staging/migrate-job-parts.ts for the focused migration. staging/verify-job-parts.mjs seeds only a labeled synthetic part/job and verifies browser add, increase, reduction and removal. Original Lightspeed records and live Floot stay unchanged. The shared staging identity limitation still applies. Printed job sheets now include current parts as described below; invoices remain separate work.

## Printed job sheets

Full job sheets in standalone staging load the saved job-part snapshots and show part number, supplier, description and exact quantity. Zero-quantity returned lines are omitted; jobs with no current parts say so explicitly. Prices and costs are not printed. The compact checklist remains unchanged. Both print routes share this layout.

The sheet waits for its authorized, uncached parts read before becoming printable. A failed read shows an error instead of an incomplete job sheet. Printing only reads records; it never issues or returns stock. Tables repeat column headings across printed pages, wrap long descriptions and keep individual rows together.

Use `staging/verify-job-parts-print.mjs --seed` with the staging URL/login environment to prepare 19 synthetic parts and a dedicated synthetic job: 18 issued quantities of 2.5 and one returned line. After browser/print-preview checks, `--verify` compares line versions, balances and history with the private baseline; `--cleanup` returns all synthetic stock. No Lightspeed parts are modified.
