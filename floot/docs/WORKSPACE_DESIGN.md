# Workspace appearance

The **Layout** menu in staging switches between two arrangements. **Sidebar** keeps grouped, collapsible navigation on the left with rounded controls and a subtle dotted workspace background. **Top navigation** moves the same destinations into dropdowns along the top, uses tighter spacing and lower-radius controls with a fine beveled edge, and uses the existing Lakeview aerial photo on the gateway. Both layouts keep the same labels and actions. Narrow screens collapse navigation under **Menu**, stack form controls, and keep wide tables scrollable inside their panels.

Shared field heights and spacing apply to Administration, customer and unit forms, parts inventory, Service Desk filters and dialogs, and mapping toolbars. Buttons distinguish primary actions from secondary controls, and audit statuses combine color and text. Embedded workspaces have their own internal control systems.

The **Theme** menu in the workspace header offers three light themes (Paper, Mist, Sand), three dark themes (Midnight, Graphite, Pine), and three [daisyUI palette previews](https://daisyui.com/docs/themes/) (Corporate, Nord, Night). The previews adapt the daisyUI 5.8.2 base colors to Marine Ops surfaces and adjust action colors for contrast. Palette values are shared by the company workspace, Administration, customers/units, inventory, Service Desk, dialogs and gateway shell. Branding images retain white backing where needed for their original artwork. Embedded third-party tools and mapping imagery are not recolored.

Theme and layout preferences are stored independently under `marineops.appearance.v1` and `marineops.layout.v1`, applied before React renders, retained across navigation/reload, and synchronized across tabs. Invalid or unavailable storage falls back to Paper and Sidebar. Print pages omit the toolbar and use white paper with dark text.

`node --test tests/appearance.test.mjs` verifies all nine palettes maintain at least 4.5:1 contrast for body/secondary/action text across their surfaces and for primary button labels. CI includes these checks with the frontend build and standalone startup tests. Staging retains its isolation banner; live Floot, main and Outlook synchronization are unchanged.
