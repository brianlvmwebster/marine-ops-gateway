Local scheduling supports new jobs, edits, notes, photos, completion/signatures, schedule administration, history, JSON exports and printing. New appointments check the configured booking window and local occupied times, serializing bookings on the schedule row; identical submission IDs retry without duplication. Imported appointments without a stored duration reserve a conservative two-hour interval. Job edits retain optimistic revision checks. Deletion soft-deletes the local copy and retains history without touching any historical Outlook event. Original Floot production remains independent; new staging writes are not mirrored back. Production authentication and external-provider setup remain outside this staging deployment.
